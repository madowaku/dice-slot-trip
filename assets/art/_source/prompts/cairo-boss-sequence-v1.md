## Shared visual specification

Use case: `stylized-concept`

Asset type: DICE SLOT TRIP game boss-sequence illustration for a 4:3 in-game overlay.

Scene: ancient Cairo temple courtyard at blue-hour night, with carved sandstone,
pyramids, warm lanterns and a circular golden boss gate.

Characters: exactly one orange-and-cream explorer cat and exactly one much larger
limestone sleepy-sphinx cat. Preserve the explorer cat's tan pith helmet with teal
goggles, teal scarf, beige expedition outfit and brown backpack. Preserve the
sphinx's round friendly stone-cat face, cracked limestone texture, muted
teal-and-antique-gold striped Egyptian headdress, forehead medallion and large
resting forepaws.

Style: polished hand-painted 3D storybook game illustration; tactile sandstone
and cloth; charming, family-friendly adventure; readable at small mobile size.

Constraints: no redesign; no text, letters, numbers, UI, health bars, card frame,
logo or watermark; no extra characters; no duplicate cat, sphinx or die; no
weapons, horror or photorealism.

## Scene 1 — Boss start

The explorer cat reaches the Cairo boss chamber. The sleepy sphinx awakens for a
friendly but dramatic dice challenge, opening its eyes with a calm golden glow.
Place exactly one ivory six-sided die on the floor between them. Use a low-angle
confrontation that makes the sphinx imposing but never frightening. Deep teal
night and warm gold lantern light create an exciting boss-introduction climax.

## Scene 2 — Victory

Continue directly from scene 1. The explorer cat wins the challenge and celebrates
with both feet on the ground, one paw raised. The sphinx lowers its head in a
gracious, approving bow, closes its eyes with a proud smile and opens the gate
into warm sunrise-colored light. Keep the sphinx unharmed and unbroken. The mood
is earned triumph, gratitude and invitation to continue the journey.

## Scene 3 — Near miss

Continue directly from scene 1. The explorer cat narrowly loses and rests on one
knee beside the single settled die, unhurt, adjusting its slightly askew helmet
while looking up with renewed determination. The sphinx watches kindly and
extends one forepaw in encouragement. The gate is closing, but a narrow warm line
of light remains. The mood is a quiet near-miss and promise of another try, never
bleak, frightening or humiliating.
